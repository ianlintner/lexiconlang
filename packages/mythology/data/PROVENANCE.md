# Provenance — @lexiconlang/mythology

All data in `mythology.pack.json` is **original work** authored for this pack and
dedicated to the public domain under [CC0-1.0](./LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt). It contains
no copied corpus text, no real living people, no trademarks and no setting-specific
intellectual property.

## Naming-style framing

The five name banks are *inspired by* broad, public-domain language families and
historical naming conventions:

| Culture | Inspired-by framing | Relationship to B1 bases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hellenic` | romanized Ancient Greek | extends `lexiconlang.bases.greek` |
| `norse` | Old Norse | extends `lexiconlang.bases.norse` |
| `sylvan` | Old Irish / Gaelic | extends `lexiconlang.bases.celtic` |
| `riverland` | ancient Egyptian naming | self-contained (no B2 base yet) |
| `mesoamerican` | Nahuatl naming | self-contained (no B2 base yet) |

Every form is invented; glosses describe the invented elements, not attested
historical meanings. No name is claimed to be an authentic deity name, historical
figure, liturgical formula or sacred/restricted name. Names resembling real
mythological figures or places were deliberately altered (for example `Thor` → `Thorn`,
`Quetzalcoatl` → `Quetzlcoatl`, `Karnak` → `Karnakh`).

## Sensitivity

- No sacred, liturgical or restricted-use names are reproduced.
- No deities of living religions are represented; all divine names are fictional.
- Cultural framing is explicitly "inspired-by, fictional, simplified".
- Review status: context-sensitive. Consumers presenting these names should retain
  the inspired-by framing and avoid implying anthropological authenticity.

## Provenance entries

Each pack-level `provenance` entry records the original author ("LexiconLang
contributors"), the CC0-1.0 license and the `modifications: "original invented
content; no external source"` note. No external files were retrieved.
