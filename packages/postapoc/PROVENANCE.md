# Provenance

All data in `src/postapoc.pack.json` (names, settlement parts, faction words, mutation, gear,
vehicle, trade-good and encounter tables, bunker designations) is original synthetic material
authored for lexiconlang and released under CC0-1.0. No third-party corpora, trademarks, real
people or setting-specific IP were used. The same statement is carried machine-readably in the
pack's `provenance` array.
