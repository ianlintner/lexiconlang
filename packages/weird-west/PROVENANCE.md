# Provenance

All data in `data/weird-west.pack.json` (word lists, grammars, tables, the two invented
cultures `dustborn` and `saltmarch` with their glossed vocabularies) is original material authored
for lexiconlang and released under CC0-1.0. No third-party corpora, no real people, no
trademarked or setting-specific names were used. `dustborn` and `saltmarch` are invented
tongues; they do not represent any real people or language. The same statement is carried
machine-readably in the pack's `provenance` array.
