The two culture templates are inspired by broad Old-English and European
city-name conventions. They are invented strings with literal English glosses;
they are not reconstructions of historical languages or claims of cultural
authenticity. The pack declares `language-v1`, `grammar-v1` and `tables-v1`
capabilities, and depends on no other pack.
