The two culture templates are invented strings with literal English glosses.
The Ashbound style draws on broad dark-fantasy Germanic naming conventions;
the Vermin ratfolk style uses invented chittering morphemes for clan and
warlord naming. Neither is a reconstruction of a historical language or a
copy of a commercial setting. The pack declares `language-v1`, `grammar-v1`
and `tables-v1` capabilities, and depends on no other pack.
