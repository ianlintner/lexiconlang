The two culture templates are fictional modern naming styles with literal
English glosses; they are invented strings and are not reconstructions of any
real language or claims of cultural authenticity. Hero and villain codenames,
power descriptions, origins, teams, lairs, headlines and oracle entries are
original synthetic fiction with no real-world identities, trademarks or borrowed
setting IP. The pack declares `language-v1`, `grammar-v1` and `tables-v1`
capabilities, and depends on no other pack.
