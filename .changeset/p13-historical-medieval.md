Add the P13 `historical-medieval` genre pack at 0.1.0: a declarative pack JSON
with four original naming cultures (Latin, Old English, Celtic, Norse), manors,
guilds, monasteries, feudal titles, market goods and prices, domain events, and
a heraldry generator emitting a blazon string plus a simple SVG shield. Ships a
thin typed `withSeed(seed)` facade, schema validation tests, docs, an example,
and CC0 provenance.
