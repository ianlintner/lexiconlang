Add `@lexiconlang/steampunk` 0.1.0: declarative F2 pack with two translated naming styles, 14 generators for steampunk/gaslamp people, places, airships, contraptions, guilds, societies, headlines, districts, automatons, patent titles, streets, official titles and two oracle tables. Includes schema validation, determinism and distinctness tests, docs and a runnable cross-genre example.
