Add `@lexiconlang/supers`, a declarative genre pack for masked-hero fiction:
hero and villain codenames, power sets with 2d6 limits, origins, teams and
organizations, two modern glossed naming styles for secret identities, lairs,
headlines and incident/omen oracles. Includes a typed `withSeed` facade,
determinism and distinctness tests, docs-site page and cross-genre example.
