Add `@lexiconlang/wuxia` 0.1.0: declarative F2 pack with two glossed naming styles (pinyin-style and translated English) and 12 generators for wuxia/xianxia people, places, sects, martial techniques, cultivation realms, treasures, jianghu nicknames, mountains, secret realms, pills and two oracle tables. Includes schema validation, determinism and distinctness tests, docs and a runnable cross-genre example.
